About DjangoBlog

DjangoBlog is a multi-user blogging platform built with Django, PostgreSQL and Bootstrap 5.

What you can do

  • Create an account and sign in or out securely.
  • Write, edit and delete your own posts, with an optional cover image.
  • Save posts as drafts that only you can see, then publish them when ready.
  • Organize content with categories and tags.
  • Reset your password by e-mail.

Built with

Django 5 · PostgreSQL · Bootstrap 5 · Pillow · WhiteNoise
